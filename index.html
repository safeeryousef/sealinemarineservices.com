About Us

Marine specialists, built on quality and reliability

  • 2021Established
  • L.L.CLicensed in Abu Dhabi
  • Mon–SatWorkshop open

Established in 2021, Sea Line Marine Services L.L.C – S.P.C is a licensed Abu Dhabi company providing marine and oil industry services from Musaffah. Our team of skilled technicians is passionate about boats and the oil industry, and trains regularly to stay current with the latest technologies and methods.

We serve private boat owners, commercial fleets, oil companies and maritime agencies – many through long-standing relationships.

  • Quality – the best materials and techniques for every repair.
  • Efficient turnaround – your equipment back in service with minimal downtime.
  • Complete service – routine maintenance, custom modifications and major repairs.
