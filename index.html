About Us

A trusted provider of marine and oil industry services

Sea Line Marine Services LLC is a leading provider of marine and oil industry services. We specialize in boat repair, maintenance and painting, jetski and jet boat servicing, fiber and carbon fiber works, as well as onshore and offshore oil and gas field services.

Founded by a team of passionate professionals with deep expertise in marine engineering and water sports, we have built a reputation for quality and customer satisfaction. Whether you need routine maintenance, performance upgrades or custom fiber solutions, we keep your equipment in optimal condition for a smooth, safe experience on the water.

Our Mission

To deliver top-quality marine and oil industry services that ensure the safety, performance and longevity of our clients' vessels and equipment – exceeding expectations through expertise, professionalism and dedication.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of marine services in the region, recognized for our expertise, customer service and innovation in delivering tailored solutions.
