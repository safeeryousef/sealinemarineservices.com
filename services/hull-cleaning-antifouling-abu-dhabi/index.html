Hull Cleaning, Antifouling & Boat Polishing in Abu Dhabi

Hull cleaning, antifouling treatments, polishing and minor hull repairs to protect your boat and keep it performing.

Get a Quote on WhatsApp Call Now

Marine growth on the hull slows your boat, increases fuel use and damages the surface over time. We clean, treat and polish hulls, and repair minor damage, so your boat looks its best and runs efficiently.

What we do

  • Hull cleaning

    Removal of marine growth and dirt from the hull.

  • Antifouling

    Antifouling treatments to slow marine growth.

  • Polishing

    Polishing to restore shine and protect the finish.

  • Minor hull repairs

    Repairs to chips, scratches and minor damage.

  • Gel coat touch-ups

    Gel coat repair and touch-ups where needed.

  • Preventive maintenance

    Regular care to keep your boat in top condition.
