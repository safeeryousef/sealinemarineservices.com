Frequently asked questions

Which jetski brands do you work on?

We work on all major brands, including Yamaha, Sea-Doo and Kawasaki.

How do I get a quote?

Send us your jetski model and a description of the problem on WhatsApp – photos or a short video help. We'll inspect it and give you a clear quote before any work starts.

Do you service jet boats, Super Jets and flyboards too?

Yes. We service and repair jet boats, Super Jets and flyboards as well as sit-down jetskis.
